# FOOD FRESHNER DETECTOR
A Food Freshener Detector is a device or system designed to check the freshness of food.

It helps identify spoiled or unsafe food before consumption.

The detector may use sensors to monitor smell, color, texture, or chemical changes.

It provides quick feedback, indicating whether food is fresh, about to spoil, or already spoiled.

Such detectors improve food safety, reduce waste, and ensure healthier consumption
